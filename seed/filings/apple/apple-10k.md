## Item 1. Business

Apple Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets,
wearables, and accessories, and sells a range of related services. The Company's products
include iPhone, Mac, iPad, and Wearables, Home and Accessories. Services include advertising,
AppleCare, cloud services, digital content, and payment services.

The Company sells through its retail and online stores and direct sales force, as well as
third-party cellular network carriers, wholesalers, retailers, and resellers. It competes on
the basis of product performance, innovation, ease of use, and an integrated ecosystem.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

- **Macroeconomic conditions.** Global and regional economic conditions, including inflation
  and currency volatility, could materially and adversely affect demand and margins.
- **Competition.** The markets for the Company's products and services are highly competitive
  and subject to rapid technological change and aggressive pricing.
- **Supply concentration.** A significant portion of manufacturing is performed by a small
  number of outsourcing partners, often concentrated in single locations, creating
  concentration risk.
- **Foreign exchange.** A large share of revenue is generated outside the United States;
  strengthening of the U.S. dollar reduces reported international revenue.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)

Total net sales were **$391.0 billion**, up **6%** year over year. Products revenue was
$294.9 billion and Services revenue reached a record **$96.1 billion**, up 13%, driven by
advertising, cloud, and payment services. Gross margin expanded to **46.2%** on a richer
Services mix. Operating income was $123.2 billion. The Company returned approximately
**$95 billion** to shareholders through dividends and share repurchases during the year.
