Company: Microsoft Corporation · Ticker: MSFT · Exchange: NASDAQ · Fiscal year end: June 30

## Item 1. Business

Microsoft develops and supports software, services, devices, and solutions. The Company reports
results in three segments:

- **Productivity and Business Processes**: Microsoft 365, Teams, Exchange, and LinkedIn.
- **Intelligent Cloud**: Azure and other cloud services, server products, and enterprise
  services.
- **More Personal Computing**: Windows, devices, gaming, and search advertising.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

- **Cloud execution.** Continued growth depends on scaling Azure capacity, including AI
  infrastructure, ahead of demand while managing capital expenditure.
- **Competition.** The Company faces intense competition across cloud, productivity, gaming,
  and search from well-capitalized rivals.
- **Cybersecurity.** Security incidents affecting cloud services could harm reputation, result
  in liability, and reduce customer trust.
- **Regulatory.** Antitrust, data-protection, and AI-related regulation across jurisdictions may
  constrain the business.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A)

Total revenue was **$245.1 billion**, up **15%** year over year. Intelligent Cloud revenue was
**$105.4 billion**, up 20%, led by Azure. Productivity and Business Processes revenue was
$77.6 billion. Gross margin was **69.8%**. Operating income was **$109.4 billion**. Capital
expenditure rose materially to support AI and cloud infrastructure buildout.
